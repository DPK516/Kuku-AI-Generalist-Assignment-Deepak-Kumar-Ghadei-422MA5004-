Continuity Guard

For the story editor who signs off a batch of AI-generated microdrama scripts before they go to video and voice generation. It catches the same character, place or prop being named differently across episodes, and flags facts that contradict each other. Each issue comes with episode and timestamp evidence, plus a canonical story bible the pipeline can use to keep faces, voices and names consistent.

Story bible

How it works

Rules (code, free, instant)

Groups near-identical proper nouns (Ashburn / Ashburton…) and speaker labels for one person (JULIANA / LADY JULIANA), with every occurrence as evidence. It knows LADY X and LORD X are two people.

Fact ledger (AI)

Reads episodes in batches of 10 and records who is who, aliases, places, props, status changes and timeline facts, each with an exact quote.

Reconcile + verify

AI compares the ledgers for contradictions. Code then checks every quote against the real script. Issues whose evidence can't be found are marked unverified and sorted last.
